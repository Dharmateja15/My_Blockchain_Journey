# Blockchain Terms & Definitions

This file contains important blockchain terminology I learn throughout my blockchain journey.

---

## Blockchain

A decentralized and distributed digital ledger that records transactions in blocks linked together using cryptography.

---

## Decentralization

A system where control is distributed among many participants instead of a single central authority.

---

## Bitcoin (BTC)

The first cryptocurrency created by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2008.

Its primary purpose is to enable peer-to-peer digital payments without intermediaries.

Maximum supply: 21 million BTC.

---

## Ethereum

A blockchain platform that allows developers to build decentralized applications and smart contracts.

Ethereum extends blockchain functionality beyond digital currency.

---

## Smart Contract

A program stored on a blockchain that automatically executes when predefined conditions are met.

Example:

"If payment is received, transfer ownership."

---

## Cryptocurrency

A digital asset secured by cryptography and operated on a blockchain network.

Examples:

* Bitcoin (BTC)
* Ether (ETH)

---

## Stablecoin

A cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value.

Example:

USDC aims to maintain a value close to 1 USD.

---

## USDC

A stablecoin backed by reserves and designed to track the value of the US Dollar.

---

## Web3

The next generation of the internet focused on decentralization, user ownership, and blockchain technology.

---

## dApp (Decentralized Application)

An application that runs on a blockchain network instead of a centralized server.

Example:

A decentralized exchange running on Ethereum.

---

## Consensus

The process through which blockchain participants agree on the valid state of the network.

---

## Double Spending Problem

The risk that digital money could be copied and spent multiple times.

Bitcoin solves this problem using blockchain and network consensus.

---

## Hash

A unique fixed-length output generated from data using a hashing algorithm.

Small changes in input produce completely different hashes.

---

## Block

A container that stores transaction data and is linked to previous blocks through hashes.

---

## Ledger

A record of transactions maintained by the blockchain network.

---

## Satoshi Nakamoto

The pseudonymous creator of Bitcoin and author of the Bitcoin Whitepaper.
